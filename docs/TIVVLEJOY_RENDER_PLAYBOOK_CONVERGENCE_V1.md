# TIVVLEJOY_RENDER_PLAYBOOK_CONVERGENCE_V1

`TIVVLEJOY_DIRECTOR_TO_RENDER_PACKAGE_V1` connects a fresh Final Shot Spec
to existing Shot Assembly, Blender readiness, and Render Backend Readiness.

Required:

- fresh `finalShotSpecSha256`
- fresh animation manifest
- fresh scenery resolution
- fresh visual approval
- render preflight

Never issued here: paid authorization, RunPod mutation, GPU launch.

Ladder (planning only): `PLANNING`, `BLOCKING_PREVIEW`,
`ANIMATION_PREVIEW`, `LIGHTING_PREVIEW`, `DIRECTOR_PREVIEW`,
`FINAL_PREFLIGHT`, `FINAL`.

`FINAL` remains paid and human-authorized. Cost forecasts return estimated
frames, minutes, compute hours, cost, confidence, and assumptions.
`authorizationIssued=false`. Caller-supplied rates only.
