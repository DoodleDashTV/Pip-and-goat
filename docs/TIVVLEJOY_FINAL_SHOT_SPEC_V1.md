# TIVVLEJOY_FINAL_SHOT_SPEC_V1

A Final Shot Spec is the single director-approved technical description of a
shot. It is metadata. `mediaExecuted=false`.

## References

Story beat, camera, staging, animation manifest, environment, lighting, VFX,
dialogue, SFX, music, caption, continuity, shot assembly, QC requirements.

Hash: `finalShotSpecSha256`.

## Change impact

- Camera change: camera, composition QC, staging visibility, render. Voice
  receipt and scenery source inspection stay valid.
- Dialogue timing: viseme, performance timing, edit, caption, audio. Scenery
  source stays valid.
- Lighting: lighting, visual approval, render. Voice receipt stays valid.
- Caption edit: caption plan only. Animation and scenery stay valid.
