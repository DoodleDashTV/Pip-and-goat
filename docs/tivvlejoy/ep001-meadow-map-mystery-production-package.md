## Story

Pip and Goat chase a loose map fragment from their Neighborhood Shops home into a meadow. Goat searches low, Pip looks high, and together they recover the missing corner. When the pieces touch, the Lost Doodle Map draws its first new path into the Enchanted Outskirts.

Theme: Different viewpoints become a strength when friends work together.
