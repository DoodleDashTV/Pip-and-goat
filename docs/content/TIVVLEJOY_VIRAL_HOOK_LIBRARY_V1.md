# TIVVLEJOY_VIRAL_HOOK_LIBRARY_V1

Checkpoint: `TIVVLEJOY_VIRAL_HOOK_LIBRARY_V1`

Internal catalog name only. User-facing language is **Hook Library**,
**first meaningful visual event**, and **positive arousal hook**.

This is not a guarantee of virality. No individual study proves a sharing
formula. TivvleJoy does not calculate a numerical reach score, guaranteed-view
number, or other scientifically misleading composite.

Operator UI: `/audience-engagement`

## What a hook is

The first meaningful visual event in the episode.

Allowed intended emotions:

- delight
- awe
- laughter
- curiosity
- excitement

Blocked intended emotions: fear, anger, anxiety, or any other
negative-emotion manipulation.

The hook must be concrete, readable in 9:16, and kind. Characters communicate
safety through face and body.

## How to use a card

Each card is planning-only. It is not a final script, paid voice line, or
approved production asset.

Copy the card into `positiveArousalHook` on an Audience Engagement Blueprint.
Then complete visible goal, causal chain, and the rest of
`TIVVLEJOY_RESEARCH_INFORMED_KIDS_ENGAGEMENT_V1`.

## Pattern bank

Reusable first images, not promises of reach:

- A familiar street object suddenly matches the map.
- A friendly light, steam, or sparkle points at the goal object.
- A small creature or cloud asks for help without danger.
- A harmless parade or puff of pastry appears after a mischievous press.
- A hidden corner, tag, or footprint waits for a second look.

Do not use jumpscares, injury gags, humiliation, or endless fake-out loops.
