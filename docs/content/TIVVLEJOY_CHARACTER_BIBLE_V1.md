Voice-guide “enthusiastic” is the locked delivery of Pip’s energetic story
trait. Do not rewrite the voice arrays.

## Equal importance

Pip and Goat share emotional weight in every episode.

- Alternate who discovers, complicates, and solves.
- Goat is never consistently stupid and never only the victim.
- Occasional mischief is allowed. Cruelty, humiliation, and cowardice are not.
- Do not make Pip mean, adult, or a lecturer.

## What each character is for

Pip notices. Pip names the clue in a short, kind sentence. Pip keeps the
goal object readable.

Goat steadies. Goat checks the map, backs Pip up, and may press a button or
fold a map the wrong way without becoming the joke of the show.

Together they repair, share, and finish the visible goal.

## Forbidden portrayals

- Fear, anger, or anxiety used to hook the audience
- Injury, pain, or humiliation comedy
- Adult, shrill, gravelly, growling, or babyish voices
- Imitating a bleat for Goat
- Removing identity accessories
- Rewriting approved spoken lines

## Age-band acting

Ages 5–7 need strong readable expressions, short sentences, and literal
cause and effect.

Ages 8–10 may get a clever callback or light wordplay. That layer cannot hide
a required plot fact from younger viewers.
