# TIVVLEJOY_RECURRING_CHARACTERS_V1

Checkpoint: `TIVVLEJOY_RECURRING_CHARACTERS_V1`

Recurring faces support familiarity and replay. They do not replace Pip and
Goat, and they do not unlock a second founding pair.

Operator UI: `/audience-engagement` · `/characters`

## Locked leads

Pip (`CHAR_PIP_001`) and Goat (`CHAR_GOAT_001`) are the only founding
characters. See `docs/content/TIVVLEJOY_CHARACTER_BIBLE_V1.md`.

Every episode keeps them equally important. Supporting faces may help, hide,
or be helped. They do not steal the visible goal.

## Recurring object-character

The Lost Doodle Map behaves like a familiar partner, not a person.

- Readable in inserts
- Can be incomplete, folded, or dusty
- Never speaks over Pip and Goat
- Never becomes a jump scare

Do not invent a large guest cast that requires new locked voices.

## How guests enter a story

1. One readable job: hide a clue, need help, or receive a kindness.
2. Strong expression. Short optional sentence. No story-critical wordplay
   for ages 5–7.
3. Pip or Goat still discovers, complicates, or solves.
4. The guest does not collect comments, names, or other child information.
5. The guest is reusable only after a human keeps them.

## Forbidden guest patterns

- Comic-relief character who is only stupid or only injured
- Frightening antagonist whose peril is disguised as humor
- Guest who replaces Goat as the loyal partner
- Guest who requires a new paid voice in a planning task
