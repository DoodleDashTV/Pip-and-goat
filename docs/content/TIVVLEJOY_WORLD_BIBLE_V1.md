# TIVVLEJOY_WORLD_BIBLE_V1

Checkpoint: `TIVVLEJOY_WORLD_BIBLE_V1`

In-fiction world: **Pip & Goat and the Lost Doodle Map**.

This bible describes reusable places and props. It does not purchase scenery,
open commercial bytes, or replace World Builder software.

Operator UI: `/world-builder` · `/episode-planner` · `/audience-engagement`

## Story purpose

The world is a readable map that children can follow. One hero object stays
visually trackable. New places enrich older viewers without confusing ages 5–7.

Home is safe. Adventure is a short walk that still feels like a journey.
Discovery is one new readable fact, not a frightening ordeal.

## World nodes

| Node | District | Feeling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HOME_NEIGHBORHOOD | home / shops | Safe start. Bakery, shops, village street. |
| ENCHANTED_OUTSKIRTS | forest edge | Adjacent hop from home. Marked trees, path, gentle wonder. |
| WATERFRONT_DISTRICT | river | Quieter path, crossings, water that stays safe. |
| AMUSEMENT_PARK | plaza | Optional spectacle. Keep peril gentle. |
| SKY_GATE | magical zone | Later layered inference only. Never a fear hook. |
| CITY_IN_THE_SKY | magical zone | Later world. Do not require it for first episodes. |

Non-adjacent hops need an explicit transition.

## Location presets

Reuse these existing presets before inventing a new place:

- `home_village` — village square / residential lane
- `main_street` — market street, Neighborhood Shops home base
- `bakery` — bakery exterior or interior, readable sign
- `map_shop` — shop exterior or interior, map-shop sign
- `forest_exit` — forest path / marked-tree reveal
- `river_road` — riverbank
- `amusement_entrance` — amusement plaza

Neighborhood Shops is the bakery / map-shop / main-street cluster, not a new
purchased pack.

## Hero prop

The Lost Doodle Map is the recurring story object.

- Keep it readable in 9:16 inserts.
- A missing piece, flour trail, or folded corner is a visual goal.
- Do not replace it with a different franchise MacGuffin.

Recurring motifs that may support a beat: Pip, Goat, map, portal, discovery.
Do not add random noise or competing moving decorations.

## Lighting and time

Reuse native presets: morning warm, day adventure, golden hour, overcast soft,
rainy cozy, evening festival, magical night.

A bakery morning should not repeat until it becomes the only look of the
season. See scenery longevity planning.

## Safety

- Planning and Preview only unless a later human gate says otherwise.
- No commercial scenery access from this document.
- New user-facing copy stays TivvleJoy-only.
- The world never collects information from children.
