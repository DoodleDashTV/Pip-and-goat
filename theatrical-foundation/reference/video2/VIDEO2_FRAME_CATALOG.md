## Observed notes (intake only, not a rebuild spec)

These notes record what the frames show. They are not an instruction to rebuild yet.

- **Scale is inconsistent across composites.** Pip reads from about half Goat height (VIDEO2_FRAME_01) to roughly two-thirds, to nearly peer-sized in some rear pairs. Catalog the range. Do not pick a scale yet.
- **Backpacks:** Pip wears a purple pack (straps, flap, side pockets; sometimes gold star or zipper accents). Goat wears a blue pack (buckles, side pouches; sometimes a dark harness read). Both packs sit large on the bodies.
- **Goat collar:** blue collar with a circular gold tag. Inscribed text is observed as DOTT or GOAT depending on the panel. Record the variance; do not lock a tag legend yet.
- **Blue footprints:** emissive cyan. Shape varies: five-toed humanoid, four-toed pads, or soft puddle-like splashes. Interiors often sparkle like stardust. They light the dirt and the characters' lower legs. In some shots the pair follows prints already on the path; in others prints appear under their own feet.
- **Forest:** Whispering Woods. Massive trunks, ferns, moss, mushrooms, dirt-and-stone path, trees leaning into a tunnel. Rustic wooden sign with carved lettering. Warm lantern vs cool blue-green forest shadow.
- **Atmosphere:** cinematic night adventure. Warm lantern against cool woods. Watching eyes and a hulking monster silhouette appear in later frames. Catalog as video context. Do not rebuild a frightening background presence until Justin gives an explicit reconstruct instruction.
- **Watermarks:** KlingAI 1.0 / 3.0 on several panels. Source marks only.
