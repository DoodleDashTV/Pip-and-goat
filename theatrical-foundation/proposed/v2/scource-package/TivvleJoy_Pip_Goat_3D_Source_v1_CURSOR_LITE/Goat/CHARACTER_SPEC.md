# Goat (`CHAR_GOAT_001`) — Visual Contract

## Identity

Goat is Pip's warm, playful young male explorer companion. He has a friendly childlike face, large amber eyes, an upright biped silhouette, long expressive ears, and explorer neckwear with a compass.

## Binding visible features

- Cream/beige coat with soft stylized fur direction.
- Warm brown facial patch around his left eye (viewer-right in front view), plus warm brown shoulder/upper-back markings.
- Two dark brown ridged horns with readable segmentation and gentle outward curvature.
- Large amber/golden eyes, pale sclera, black pupils, strong catchlights, and expressive brows.
- Long drooping ears with warm pink interiors.
- Pink-brown nose and dark muzzle/mouth line with a friendly expression.
- Burnt-orange neckerchief tied behind the neck.
- Teal-rimmed compass pendant on a small brass/copper loop at the front.
- Dark split hooves on hands and feet.
- Small cream tail; preserve its placement and silhouette.

## Proportion and silhouette

- Oversized rounded head with narrow neck and compact childlike torso.
- Upright biped stance with arms relaxed at sides in neutral views.
- Horns and ears must remain distinct in silhouette from all principal angles.
- Front/back/left/right silhouettes must remain recognizable without texture.

## Materials

Aim for polished theatrical stylization: soft directional coat with controlled clumping, clean wet eye highlights, subtly textured horns and hooves, fabric neckerchief, and a readable metal/enamel compass. Avoid plastic fur, excessive photoreal strands, or muddy facial markings.

## Rig requirements for a later authorized phase

Independent eye aim; upper/lower eyelids; brows; muzzle, lips, jaw, smile, and phoneme shapes; ear and horn-safe head controls; neck/head squash-and-stretch; arm, split-hoof hand, leg, and foot controls; tail, ears, neckerchief, and pendant secondary motion. These requirements are not authorization to rig now.
