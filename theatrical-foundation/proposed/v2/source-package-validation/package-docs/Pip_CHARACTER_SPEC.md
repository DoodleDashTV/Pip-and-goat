# Pip (`CHAR_PIP_001`) — Visual Contract

## Identity

Pip is a cheerful young female explorer bird. Her appeal comes from a large expressive head and eyes, a warm open smile, compact rounded body, readable feather layering, and practical teal explorer accessories.

## Binding visible features

- Yellow/chartreuse feathered body with a lighter warm face and chest.
- Three prominent coral-orange crest feathers; preserve their count, size hierarchy, and swept arrangement.
- Very large green/teal irises with black pupils, bright catchlights, pale sclera, upper lashes, and dark expressive brows.
- Small orange beak with visible upper/lower separation and an expressive mouth interior.
- Layered feather wings capable of functioning as expressive arms.
- Teal neckerchief tied at the front.
- Centered teal/forest-green backpack on the back, with two symmetrical shoulder straps. No satchel, no cross-body strap, and no hip bag.
- Slim orange-brown legs and stylized bird feet. Preserve a clear three-forward-toe read; treat rear-toe ambiguity in generated views as a review item.
- Small warm-yellow/orange layered tail feathers.

## Proportion and silhouette

- Oversized rounded head; compact pear/egg-shaped torso.
- Wings extend low enough to read as arms but retain layered feather silhouette.
- Backpack sits centered on the back between the wings; two straps pass over the shoulders under the scarf.
- Front/back/left/right silhouettes must remain recognizable without texture.

## Materials

Aim for polished theatrical stylization: soft feather response with readable sculpted layers, clean moist eye highlights, restrained beak/leg roughness, fabric-like neckerchief and backpack, and small gold backpack hardware. Avoid plastic body feathers, noisy photoreal fuzz, or flat texture-only feather detail.

## Rig requirements for a later authorized phase

Independent eye aim; upper/lower eyelids; brows; beak open/close plus smile and phoneme shaping; cheek/face deformation; neck/head squash-and-stretch controls; wing posing and feather-safe deformation; individual leg/foot controls; backpack/strap and neckerchief secondary motion; crest and tail secondary motion. These requirements are not authorization to rig now.
