FIX 1 — GOAT WHITE FOREHEAD SPOTS
Exact cause: Goat_Catch_0 and Goat_Catch_1 were detached emissive white
highlight spheres (~0.018 diameter) placed above the brows, not in the
eyes. Goat_Cornea_0 / Goat_Cornea_1 were oversized glassy spheres
(originally 0.136 diameter) whose upper hemispheres poked through the
brow as white circles.
How removed: catch spheres deleted; cornea objects kept in the file but
hidden from render (not deleted); no paint-over. Painted in-eye
catchlights remain.
Proof: front close-up brow/forehead pixel analysis white_count = 0.

FIX 2 — GOAT BROWN BACK RECTANGLE
Exact cause: Stage 2 Color stamp used a hard-z-cut teardrop (widest at a
flat top) plus a separate vertical tail-base bar at z 0.92–1.16, width
0.065. That rasterized as a brown rectangle down the back and above the
tail.
How reshaped: restored the original working Color map, then painted a
rounded-crown organic teardrop only. No tail bar. No hard z-cut.
Result: the tail rectangle is gone. The upper-back cinnamon is present
and centered under the scarf, but it still reads as a somewhat geometric
stamp, not the sheet’s fluffy teardrop. Further albedo stamps were
stopped to avoid more UV rectangles.
