## Same-film test

Both candidates come from the same Prism/Tripo high-detail family. Eye gloss,
generated surface density, accessory craft, and Khronos PBR Neutral response
are compatible. Neither character is a primitive kitbash beside a detailed
sculpt.

## Laterality

- Pip satchel: front, three-quarter, and pair reads strap over
  character-right (−Y) and bag on character-left (+Y). Do not flip.
- Goat eye patch: character-left (+Y), viewer-right in front. Held.

## Remaining pair gaps

The binding pair relationship is preserved in scale and costume language.
Absolute body volume still reads slimmer than the sheets for both, so they
match each other more closely than they match the binding silhouettes.
That is a likeness gap, not a pair-cohesion split.
