  Pip wings stay in a stiff T-pose; acting pose is a shape-key smile, not authored performance.
  Pip face close-up shows unintegrated rectangular groom cards.
  Goat eye scale is a recognizability/appeal regression versus canonical large eyes.
  Goat front camera/scale is awkward; tag can vanish; fur cards read as a halo.
